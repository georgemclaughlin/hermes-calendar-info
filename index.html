Hermes Calendar

A personal Google Calendar integration for a locally operated Hermes assistant. It helps its owner review their schedule and create or manage calendar events through conversation.

This is an individually operated integration, not a public signup service or an official Google or Nous Research product. This website provides information only; it does not provide access to the assistant or its calendars.

Privacy policy

Effective October 15, 2026

Access and purpose

The integration uses Google's OAuth authorization to access the owner's Google Calendar. Depending on the request, it may process event titles, dates, times, descriptions, locations, attendee information, and calendar identifiers to read schedules or create, edit, and delete events. The current Google permission also permits calendar sharing and management; normal use is directed to the owner's selected calendar. Gmail, Drive, and other Google services are not authorized by this integration.

Processing and storage

The assistant and its OAuth credentials are stored on the owner's computer. Relevant calendar details and conversation content may be processed by the owner's configured cloud AI provider and messaging service to fulfill requests and deliver replies. Local conversation history and logs may retain these details. Those services handle data under their own terms and privacy policies.

Google Calendar data is used for the owner's requested assistant functions. This integration does not sell calendar data or use it for advertising.

Control and deletion

The owner can revoke the integration's authorization in Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized access; it does not delete calendar events or previously stored conversation history. The owner can delete local credentials, history, and logs, and manage retained messages or data through the relevant service's controls. Calendar events remain in Google Calendar until deleted there.

This website

This static information page contains no calendar data, login form, analytics script, or application credentials. It is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub handles hosting request data under its privacy statement.

Changes

This notice will be updated if the integration's data practices change.

Contact

For questions about this integration, contact the operator through the project repository. Do not include private calendar information, tokens, or credentials in a public issue.
